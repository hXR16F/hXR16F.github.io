About Me

Hi — I'm a hobbyist programmer and tech enthusiast from Poland. I picked up coding at age 9 out of pure curiosity, and it turned into an obsession.

I learn by building — if I don't understand something, I take it apart and build my own version. My projects span web development, automation, cybersecurity, self-hosted infrastructure, local AI, and software experimentation — often starting as small ideas and growing into fully functional tools.

Most of my work is open source and lives on GitHub where repositories have accumulated over 200 stars.
